Why this matters

Charging infrastructure should not only be numerous. It should also be distributed so that residents and urban areas receive reasonably balanced access. This atlas links service area mapping with population and area based equity indicators to make cross city differences visible at a glance.

Four city comparison Amsterdam, Milan, Naples and Palermo, based on the published ICECET 2025 study.

Methodology

From open data to spatial equity

1

Collect

City study boundary, Meta population raster and Open Charge Map station points.

2

Prepare

Clip datasets to each study area and divide API search bounds into smaller retrieval grids where needed.

3

Allocate

Create Thiessen polygons so each location is associated with its nearest charging station.

4

Measure

Intersect 1 km buffers with Thiessen zones and calculate covered population and area.

5

Assess

Use Lorenz curves and Gini coefficients to compare population and area based equity.

Data note: Two source records in Milan share identical coordinates. They remain separate station records but generate one service zone because coincident points cannot form distinct Thiessen polygons.
