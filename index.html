Why this matters

Charging infrastructure should not only be numerous. It should also be distributed so that residents and urban areas receive reasonably balanced access. This prototype links service area mapping with population and area based equity indicators.

Current release Milan prototype. Amsterdam, Naples and Palermo will be added in later versions.

Methodology

From open data to spatial equity

1

Collect

Official city boundary, Meta population raster and Open Charge Map station points.

2

Prepare

Clip all datasets to the city and divide API search bounds into smaller retrieval grids.

3

Allocate

Create Thiessen polygons so every city location is assigned to its nearest charging station.

4

Measure

Intersect 1 km buffers with Thiessen zones and calculate covered population and area.

5

Assess

Use Lorenz curves and Gini coefficients to compare population and area based equity.

Two source records in Milan share identical coordinates. They remain separate station records, but form one Thiessen service zone because coincident points cannot generate distinct polygons.
